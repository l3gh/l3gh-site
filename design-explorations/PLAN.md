# Personal site: ten directions

Audience: people meeting Leah online. Primary job: introduce her and let visitors explore images, tools and links. Copy is provisional; projects remain coming soon. Minecraft is deferred.

Pre-build review: avoid making ten versions of the same hero. Each direction changes the content hierarchy and spatial arrangement. The cobalt design uses a saturated base rather than the common dark-plus-neon treatment. The quiet page uses cool white rather than cream and clay. Numbering appears only in the concept switcher, where it identifies choices. Decorative motion is omitted. Keep all prototypes responsive, keyboard-accessible, and independent of the production pages.
